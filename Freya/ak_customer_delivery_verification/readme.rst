Customer  Delivery Verification
==========================================
An email is sent only once when a new customer places an order via Wire Transfer or Pay After Delivery. It notifies recipients defined in General Settings. No emails are sent for existing or verified customers, for other payment methods, or after “Pay After Invoice” is checked.

Configuration
=============
* Set reception email in email in general settings.
